# Chapter 1 Revision Plan

## Major Changes Needed:

### 1. **ADD: Physical toll on Cassian** (Opening scene)
- Show trembling hands from exhaustion
- Torin questioning how long since he slept
- Make it clear he's running on fumes

### 2. **KEEP & ENHANCE: Infiltration sequence**
- Show team working together with learned tactics
- Demonstrate they've gotten good at this over 3 months
- Professional, coordinated, efficient
- But brief - don't drag it out
- Shows competence before things go wrong

### 3. **CHANGE: Lyssa resists instead of cooperating**
- She activates an alarm sigil (Draven's trap)
- Reveals Draven has been tracking Cassian's pattern
- Combat-trained guards (not Hollow) ambush the team
- Draven's goal: Kill or capture Cassian to stop the severances
- This is the second trap Draven has set (first one at Arvenstead worked)

### 4. **ADD: Real consequences**
- 1-2 team members die
- Maybe: Marcus (volunteer) and Kira (volunteer) 
- 1 team member badly wounded
- 1 Hollow dies during severance (on screen, immediate)
- Total: 39 deaths across all 15 operations

### 5. **CHANGE: Severance scene becomes desperate**
- Cassian has to do it while team fights off ambush
- Draven speaks to him through the network (via Coin of Oxen)
- Taunts him: "You're destroying yourself for nothing"
- The process nearly kills Cassian—he comes closest to being hollowed himself
- Shows this method is unsustainable

### 5. **CUT: Most of Lyssa's philosophical debate** (Trim by 70%)
- Keep only the sharpest lines about Cassian becoming like Draven
- Make it feel like taunting/stalling rather than genuine debate
- Save this theme for later chapters

### 7. **CUT: Mira check-in entirely**
- Doesn't advance plot
- No actionable information
- Can be moved to a later chapter when something actually happens

### 8. **ADD: Aftermath scene**
- Cassian unconscious for 16 hours
- Torin confronts him: "You're done. This is killing you."
- Team is traumatized, questioning whether to continue
- Cassian realizes he's absorbing hollowness with each severance
- Clear setup: this method cannot continue

### 9. **CHANGE: Ending**
- Instead of "mission accomplished, on to the next one"
- End with: Cassian has lost the coin, nearly died, and is falling apart
- Torin refuses to help anymore
- Major crisis point that forces Book 2 in a new direction

## Structural Flow:

**ACT 1: The Trap** (pages 1-8)
- Infiltration showing team skill and coordination
- Cassian's exhaustion increasingly visible
- Lyssa reveals it's a trap, activates alarm
- Guards (not Hollow) attack from multiple directions
- Team fights for survival

**ACT 2: Desperate Severance** (pages 8-12)
- Cassian chooses to sever Lyssa despite the danger
- Draven taunts him through the network: "You're killing yourself"
- Process nearly kills Cassian - closest he's come to being hollow
- One Hollow dies during severance (witnessed)
- Team members fall fighting the guards

**ACT 3: Brutal Extraction** (pages 12-15)
- Fighting retreat through warehouse
- Leave wounded behind (can't save everyone)
- One team member dies covering their escape
- Barely make it out alive
- Draven proves he can strike at will

**ACT 4: Breaking Point** (pages 15-18)
- Cassian wakes after 16 hours
- Body count: 2 team members, 1 Hollow, 1 abandoned
- Torin's ultimatum: "I won't help you die"
- Realization that he's absorbing hollowness
- Setup: This method cannot continue

## What This Accomplishes:

✓ **Real stakes** - People die, method proves unsustainable
✓ **Character arc** - Shows Cassian can't keep this up
✓ **Hook** - Readers want to know what he does next
✓ **Differentiation** - NOT just another routine severance
✓ **Escalation** - Draven actively hunting him, setting traps
✓ **Consequences** - Can't undo the deaths/costs
✓ **New direction** - Forces Cassian to find a better way
✓ **Team dynamics** - Show competence, then trauma
